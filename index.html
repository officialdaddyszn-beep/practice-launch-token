Utility mechanic

Ad revenue → buyback → burn

A portion of app advertising revenue is intended to buy $PLT from the open market and permanently burn it. This is a supply mechanism, not a promise that price will rise.

App rewards

Top users can earn USDT

Top-performing users are planned to be rewarded in USDT rather than additional $PLT, keeping app rewards separate from token supply emissions.
