ROADMAP · FROM PRACTICE TO PROGRESS

Small apps first. Omnexia later.

The roadmap is a planned sequence, not a promise of dates or outcomes. Development can change as the project learns.

01
Current

$PLT presale

Build the first community and raise funds intended for the first app and early network development.

02
Next

Build Ol' Crusty

Develop the first crab-themed farming-style app and begin testing user growth and advertising revenue.

03
Expand

Apps two through six

Use lessons and resources from the first app to build the remaining planned apps.

04
Scale

Revenue engine

Develop a sustainable operating model around real users and advertising revenue.

05
Destination

Omnexia platform

When the ecosystem has enough substance to support it, move toward the larger Omnexia platform. A separate Omnexia token may be considered through a future TGE.

Important

$PLT is not the future Omnexia token.

Omnexia does not currently have its own token. If one is launched later, its terms and mechanics will be announced separately. $PLT presale contributors are intended to receive priority access to a bonus allocation, with the exact ratio to be announced closer to that event.
